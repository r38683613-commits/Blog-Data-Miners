Technical Note

DineIQ Analytics — MenuMatrix Dining Intelligence is a technical story about turning a large, connected restaurant dataset into an analytical system that can be inspected, tested, and used for decision support. The project was developed for the Data Science Intelligence Arena — Aptech by Team Data Miners (منقبي البيانات) at Al-Nasser University. This English edition follows the project's documented material. It deliberately distinguishes implemented evidence from pending work. In particular, it does not invent final Python, customer segmentation, market basket, wastage, pricing, promotion, anomaly, or dual-pipeline results that the documentation identifies as dependent on Student 3's completed implementation. The documented system begins with more than one million order-line records and a connected collection of restaurant entities. It then applies data-quality rules, cleaning, Spark processing, Spark SQL integration, feature engineering, menu classification, forecasting, and Spark MLlib evaluation before connecting the analytical work to an application layer. The central idea is simple: a useful restaurant intelligence platform should explain where its numbers came from, how they were calculated, what they mean, and what remains uncertain.
